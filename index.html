About me

My name is Elbert D. Nebres, an IT graduate from Batangas State University, Major in Network Technology. I am reaching out to introduce myself and share my interest in any web developer or programming opportunities you may have available. During my OJT, I was part of the team that built the Gender and Development (GAD) Information System, a web platform used by our university office to manage records and reports. I also help a cloud-based Point of Sale system, with real-time sales tracking, inventory, and reports, accessible from any device. In both projects, I worked with PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, contributing to the database and the system's overall functionality. Aside from coding, I also have a background in computer hardware and networking, which helps me understand how systems run end to end, not just the code side.
